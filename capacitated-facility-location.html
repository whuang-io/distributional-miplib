Capacitated Facility Location

Problem Description

Capacitated Facility Location Problem (CFLP) considers satisfying customer demands from several facilities. The facilities have startup costs and can satisfy customer demand by incurring transportation costs. Once opened, a facility can satisfy customer demand by deciding how much capacity is routed to each customer. For distributions, often problems are randomly generated according to [Cornuéjols91].

Inputs

  • \(C\): The set of customers.
  • \(F\): The set of facilities.
  • \(d_c\): Customer demand required defined for each customer \(c\).
  • \(c^s_{c,f}\): Startup cost defined for each facility \(f\) .
  • \(c^t_{c,f}\): The cost of transporting one unit from facility \(f\) to customer \(c\).
  • \(b_{f}\): Capacity budget defined for each facility \(f\).

MIP Formulation

\begin{equation} \begin{split} \text{Minimize}_{x,y} \quad & \sum_{f \in F} c^s_{f}x_f + \sum_{c \in C}\sum_{f \in F} c^t_{c,f}y_{c,f} \quad & \text{(Objective: minimize total costs)} \\ \text{subject to} \quad & \sum_{f \in F} y_{c,f} = d_c, \quad \forall c \in C \quad & \text{(Constraint 1: satisfy all demand)} \\ & \sum_{c \in C} y_{c,f} \leq b_f x_f, \quad \forall f \in F \quad & \text{(Constraint 2: respect facility capacity)} \\ & y_{c,f} \geq 0, \quad \forall c \in C, f \in F \quad & \text{(Constraint 3: non-negative shipments)} \\ & x_f \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall f \in F \quad & \text{(Constraint 4: binary decision of opening facility)} \end{split} \end{equation}
